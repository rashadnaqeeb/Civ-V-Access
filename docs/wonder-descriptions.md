# World Wonders - spoken descriptions

Spoken descriptions for the world wonders of Brave New World, written from the
game's wonder splash paintings (the WonderSplashImage column of the Buildings
tables), the full-scene concept art the wonder-completed popup shows.

Style: the wonder itself first (form, material, place in the frame), then the
surrounding scene, figures, and action, then palette and light, in about three
to five sentences scaled to how busy the scene is. The wonder's name is not
repeated because the popup already announces it. Descriptions cover only what
the painting shows; the history stays in the Civilopedia. Where the artists
staged a viewpoint or invented detail, the description says so plainly.

Written to be heard, not read. Each subject enters with an indefinite article;
"the" belongs to what is already introduced, named, or plainly implied, and
nameable landmarks are named (Pisa Cathedral, the Seine) rather than left as
bare definites. Sentences carry finite verbs: at most one short absolute
phrase each, and never a clause coordinated onto the end of one. No homophone
garden paths ("viewed from", not "seen from"). Architectural and period terms
are kept, glossed inline in plain language or anchored by a construction that
makes them clear from context. Plain punctuation, no em-dashes.

Each entry: in-game building Type, English name, the splash texture name, then
the description text. The type key is the stable identifier for turning these
into mod strings. Entries are ordered by texture name. Companion docs:
leader-descriptions.md, great-work-descriptions.md,
natural-wonder-descriptions.md, victory-screen-descriptions.md,
congress-descriptions.md, era-descriptions.md.

BUILDING_MOTHERLAND_STATUE (The Motherland Calls) has no entry. Its
WonderSplashImage (WonderConceptMotherlandStatue.dds) does not exist anywhere
in the game's files; the XML references a texture Firaxis never shipped, so
there is no art to describe and sighted players see no splash either.

## 1. BUILDING_ALHAMBRA
Alhambra, WonderConceptAlahambra.dds

From under the shade of a slender stone column, a palace courtyard opens around a long reflecting pool, the Alhambra's Court of the Myrtles. The pool runs away to an arcaded portico of rose-colored stone whose arches are mirrored in the still water. A square fortress tower rises warm against the blue sky behind. Low clipped hedges line the water's edge, a potted plant sits in the foreground, and small groups in long robes stand talking along the sunlit walkways. Bright afternoon sun fills the courtyard, white where it strikes the paving. Deep shadow lies under the colonnade.

## 2. BUILDING_ANGKOR_WAT
Angkor Wat, WonderConceptAngkorWat.dds

A cluster of tiered grey stone towers shaped like closed lotus buds rises in the background, half screened by palms under a hot pale sky. The foreground is a festival procession: a grey elephant strides through a dense crowd, with a rider on its neck and a canopied seat on its back shaded by a tall red-orange parasol. The crowd presses in on all sides in white, saffron, and plum robes; heads are wrapped, shoulders bare, and every face turns toward the elephant as it passes. The scene is sunstruck and dusty, and the stone is cool green-grey against the warm skin and cloth below.

## 3. BUILDING_BIG_BEN
Big Ben, WonderConceptBigBen.dds

A Gothic clock tower stands at night at the end of Westminster Bridge, its four-faced clock glowing amber near the top. Above the clock, the spire fades into a deep blue sky. The long front of London's Houses of Parliament stretches away to the left, windows and stonework lit warm gold by floodlight. Pinnacled towers rise at its far end. The bridge crosses the foreground with a row of glowing lamp posts. A few carriages and figures show small and dark on the roadway. The whole scene is painted in night blues against the gold of the lamps and the lit stone.

## 4. BUILDING_BOROBUDUR
Borobudur, WonderConceptBorobudur.dds

A stepped pyramid of grey volcanic stone rises out of the jungle like a terraced hill, its upper tiers ringed with small bell-shaped stupas. A larger stupa crowns the summit. Palms grow from the lower terraces and the forest presses close around its base. In the foreground a file of monks in orange and red robes climbs toward the temple along a shadowed jungle path, and birds scatter across the hazy sky above. The palette is misty grey-green, and the robes are the one strong note of color.

## 5. BUILDING_BRANDENBURG_GATE
Brandenburg Gate, WonderConceptBrandenburg.dds

A broad classical gateway of weathered sandstone stands across the back of a busy city square, raised on paired rows of heavy columns and crowned by the quadriga, the bronze four-horse chariot statue, which stands dark against a pale overcast sky. A lower colonnaded wing runs off to the right toward the surrounding stone buildings. The square in front is nineteenth-century Berlin: horse-drawn carriages rolling over the cobbles, riders, and crowds in long dark coats and top hats, with a lamp post rising at the left. The colors are sober greys and browns, and the light is flat and even.

## 6. BUILDING_BROADWAY
Broadway, WonderConceptBroadway.dds

A theater district at night in the early twentieth century, the street wet with rain and shining with reflected light. Lit marquees and signs crowd both sides of the avenue, white and red bulbs spelling out show names. One long bright theater front glows at the center of the block. Crowds in dark coats and hats fill the sidewalks and spill into the street among a few old motorcars, everyone small against the tall dark office blocks that rise into the night sky. The palette is deep blue-black broken by pools of warm white, red, and yellow electric light.

## 7. BUILDING_CN_TOWER
CN Tower, WonderConceptCNTower.dds

A slender concrete needle rises at the center of Toronto's skyline, swelling into a rounded observation pod near the top and tapering on into an antenna spire. Storm clouds are breaking around it, and shafts of sunlight pour down behind the tower through the gap, hazing the office blocks on either side. The foreground is a sunken expressway streaming with traffic, headlights and red taillights glowing in the murk between concrete embankments. The whole scene is steel grey and gold. The city is half in storm shadow, half in breaking light.

## 8. BUILDING_CHICHEN_ITZA
Chichen Itza, WonderConceptChichenItza.dds

A stepped pyramid, El Castillo, stands at the center under a burning orange sky, nine square tiers climbing to a small temple at the summit. A broad stairway runs straight up its face. A second tiered temple rises at the left edge, and smoke drifts across the sun where it hangs low and red. A plaza below is filled with a crowd of Mayans, most of them facing away: bare shoulders, feathered headdresses, raised staffs and spears. A knot of warriors stands on a stone platform to the right. Everything is steeped in rust and amber dusk light. The crowd falls into dark silhouette against the glowing stone.

## 9. BUILDING_CRISTO_REDENTOR

Beyond and far below, Rio de Janeiro spreads around the water in white drifts of buildings, with the rounded hump of Sugarloaf Mountain rising from the bay among green islands and anchored ships. Haze softens the distance, leaving the city and sea in pale blues and greys, while the statue's shoulders catch the daylight.

## 10. BUILDING_COLOSSUS
Colossus, WonderConceptColossus.dds

A bronze giant stands far off on the rocky harbor mouth, one arm raised, half dissolved in golden haze. It is more silhouette than statue against a sky the color of brass. The foreground belongs to the sea: a small sailing boat leans toward the viewer under a single broad sail, two or three crewmen visible at the stern. Other sails are scattered across the harbor. Low rocks run along the waterline at the right, and the water carries the same warm gold as the sky. The whole painting is one color, amber light and haze. ## 12. BUILDING_EIFFEL_TOWER

## 14. BUILDING_GLOBE_THEATER

## 15. BUILDING_GREAT_FIREWALL
Great Firewall, WonderConceptGreatFirewall.dds

An abstract digital scene with no building in it, a dark cyberspace rendered in greens and black. Broad curved surfaces sweep through the frame like ramparts of data, their faces tiled with glowing green binary digits, and luminous traces streak along them like traffic on a highway at night. Ghostly screens and grids hover in the murk above, hinting at circuitry and surveillance. The whole image has the look of a computer's inner world, phosphor green light on black.

## 16. BUILDING_GREAT_LIBRARY
Great Library, WonderConceptGreatLibrary.dds

A monumental classical hall fronts a sunlit square, with tall red banners hung between the columns of its long colonnade. A carved frieze runs above the columns, and broad steps spread down to the pavement. The square is viewed from a shaded portico in the foreground: a column rises at the left, a stone sphinx sits on a plinth at the right, and a white dome shows in the distance beyond. ## 17. BUILDING_GREAT_LIGHTHOUSE
Great Lighthouse, WonderConceptGreatLighthouse.dds

A tall pale tower in stacked tiers stands far off at the right, lit by a break in the storm, its beacon glowing at the top. The rest of the picture is rough sea: a line of ancient galleys under red and white striped square sails labors through heavy grey-green swells toward the light, prows curved high. The sails carry the only warm color in a painting of slate, foam, and rain.

## 18. BUILDING_GREAT_WALL

## 48. BUILDING_UFFIZI
Uffizi, WonderConceptUffizi.dds

Two long gallery wings face each other across a narrow courtyard, tall Renaissance facades of grey stone and cream plaster with statues standing in niches above the ground-floor loggia. The courtyard runs deep into the picture toward an arched opening at the far end, and a tower shows against the sky beyond it. Below, the courtyard is busy with Florentines in long dresses and dark coats, sculptures displayed on pedestals down the middle, and a red-canopied stall set up in the left foreground. Afternoon sun slants in from one side, lighting the upper stories warm while the loggia below holds cool shadow.
